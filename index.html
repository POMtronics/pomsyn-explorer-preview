About

About the explorer

Berzelius 1.0 provides searchable access to curated polyoxometalate synthesis records. It brings target compounds, reagents, operations, source references, and structured procedures together in one place.

The explorer supports research and reproducible reporting by making synthesis information easier to find, compare, and read.
